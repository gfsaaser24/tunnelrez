Windows needs the HEVC codec for this encoder. NVENC native and the CPU encoder don’t need it. Two versions exist. They are the same codec from Microsoft:
Free – HEVC Video Extensions from Device Manufacturer. Made for PC makers to preinstall. The Store may hide it or refuse to install it on some PCs.
$0.99 – HEVC Video Extensions. The normal Store version; always installs.
Try the free one first. If the Store says it isn’t available, use the $0.99 one.
